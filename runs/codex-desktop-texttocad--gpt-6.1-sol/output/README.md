# Atlas2350 Rev A

Atlas2350 is an 80 × 75 mm RP2350A development board with 4 MiB QSPI flash, 8 MiB QSPI PSRAM, an ESP32-C3 Wi-Fi/BLE UART coprocessor, microSD, USB-C, and an external protected single-cell LiPo power path. Documentation reflects the final owner decisions on **2026-11-10**. The saved native PCB and assembly exports from that board are authoritative for positions, rotations and pads; `design.json` and placement scripts can contain earlier coordinates. These documents describe the current choices; earlier alternatives in `research.md` are not the assembly specification.

**Status: complete prototype CAM package.** The final revision includes U10/C45 for switched LED power; [checks/verification.json](checks/verification.json) is **PASS with all 432 declared pin assignments checked**, **115 components / 128 footprints**, and **final ERC/DRC each report zero errors**. **Final CAM parse is PASS: 10 Gerbers, 374 PTH, 7 NPTH, and 334 filled/capped holes registered within a 0.00051 mm export-rounding tolerance**. All QSPI including FLASH_CS remains F/B only. Use the matching final source/export hashes and verification reports for the order. This is a **prototype first-article package**, with no supplier quote/stock commitment, DFM approval or physical/functional testing claimed. Initial quote quantity is **five completely assembled boards**.

Final population is **115 components / 128 footprints / 53 BOM groups**, with **13 bare H/TP features excluded**. All **432 declared pin assignments** pass the published verification. Intentional U6/J3/J6 footprint edits remain part of the released local library.

Fabrication has not started. The initial quote quantity is **five completely assembled prototype boards**, each with all 115 real BOM components fitted. ## Power and firmware contract

J1 nominal 5 V enters `VBUS_RAW`, passes F1 to `VBUS`, then U4 supplies `VSYS`. U5 supplies +3V3; MAIN_PG releases U7's +1V1 core supply. SW5 controls PWR_EN through R39, enabling U5 and U10 together. U10 disconnects and discharges LED_VDD when SW5 is off, preventing D1's latched color from remaining illuminated. The battery, charger and upstream VSYS remain connected. U5 PS/SYNC shares PWR_EN, so an enabled regulator uses automatic PWM/PFM power-save behavior.

| GP16 / EN1 | GP17 / EN2 | Input mode |
|---:|---:|---|
| 0 | 0 | USB100 at reset, approximately 90–100 mA |
| 1 | 0 | USB500 after host configuration permits it, approximately 450–500 mA |
| 0 | 1 | Resistor-programmed ILIM mode; avoid accidental selection |
| 1 | 1 | USB suspend |

R7/R41 100 kΩ pull-downs select USB100 at reset. Firmware must handle host configuration, reset, suspend and resume; no complete USB policy is supplied. In suspend without a battery, the system can lose power and revert to reset defaults. R5 3.57 kΩ programs about **250 mA nominal** charge current; system load consumes the same input budget. DPPM reduces charge before battery supplementation. ITERM is NC (default 10%, 3.3% in USB100); TMR is NC (nominal 30 min precharge / 5 h fast charge).

Use an external **protected 500 mAh 1S 4.2 V LiPo** with a **cell-mounted 10 kΩ NTC** and the custom J2 pin order. Pack, cable, SD card and debug adapters are not supplied board components. The approximately **0–50 °C charge window** depends on the actual NTC curve/tolerances; confirm the pack specification. No fixed TS bypass or board-ambient substitute is fitted. BQ24074 is not a battery protection IC.

The main rail targets operation down to **BAT 2.8 V after startup**, subject to power-path drop, load and qualification. **TPS63070 cold startup requires SYS/VIN ≥3.0 V while VOUT is below 3.0 V.** A deeply discharged pack is not guaranteed to cold-start the board at 2.8 V; this is an accepted component limitation requiring test, not a full-range startup promise. Core LDO heating and minimum-input regulator stability remain first-article qualification items.

Firmware must set RP POWMAN VREG.HIZ using the protected register-write mechanism for the external-core supply; initialize shared QMI flash/PSRAM with separate chip selects, PSRAM startup/reset and bounded transactions; configure the actual SPI1/I²C0/UART1 pin assignments; and leave ESP reset low until the power budget permits radio operation. ESP-AT UART1 defaults are GPIO6RX/7TX/5CTS/4RTS, normally 115200 baud, but depend on the flashed binary. No factory ESP-AT image is assumed.

For first-article bring-up, start QSPI at **≤31.25 MHz** and SD SPI at **≤12.5 MHz** after the card's required slower initialization sequence. These are conservative firmware recommendations pending signal-integrity qualification, not measured bus-speed guarantees. QSPI and hand-routed USB use F/B copper; no controlled-impedance claim is made. Higher bus clocks require first-article waveform/error-rate and concurrent-load validation.

RGB uses **VSYS-derived LED_VDD through U10, with direct 3.3 V GPIO data**, using the primary PDF's VIH=**0.55×VDD**. The owner accepts that the LED may fall outside its guaranteed supply range at low battery; the system rail's operating range does not guarantee RGB operation. Firmware shall blank conservatively at **BAT ≤3.6 V under load**, with calibration/hysteresis and sag assessment. Firmware blanking at low battery does not disconnect D1; SW5 off disables U10 and discharges LED_VDD through QOD. Use GRB data and a reset-low interval >280 µs; budget approximately 36 mA for full white. No 5 V LED boost or AHCT shifter is part of this design.

U10 pin map: **1 IN=VSYS, 2 GND, 3 ON=PWR_EN, 4 NC floating, 5 QOD=LED_VDD, 6 VOUT=LED_VDD**. D1/C24/C36 use LED_VDD; C45 adds 1 µF from VSYS to GND at U10 input. QOD tied directly to VOUT enables internal output discharge. The main rail remains **3.296 V**. This switch supplies no boost/regulation: RGB remains outside its guarantee if LED_VDD falls below 3.3 V. [Primary TI TPS22919 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps22919.pdf).

**U10 schematic model:** local symbol pin5 QOD is typed **passive** to represent TI's explicitly permitted direct tie to VOUT/pin6 (LED_VDD). The stock `open_collector` type produces an ERC conflict with the power-output pin6 for this permitted connection. Only the schematic electrical type is changed: pin numbers, package pinout and footprint are unchanged. **No ERC exclusions are used.** [TI TPS22919 pin-functions table](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps22919.pdf).

## Mechanical and fabrication target

Four layers: **F/B signals, power and full GND pours; In1/In2 predominantly stitched GND pours with low-speed escape corridors on both inner layers**. The inner layers are **not continuous GND planes**. Ground connectivity includes the outer pours and exposed-pad link. Construction is 1.6 mm, ENIG, outer 1 oz / inner 0.5 oz copper; signal width 0.15 mm, clearance 0.127 mm, routing via 0.60 mm land / 0.30 mm finished hole. **Every routing via requires IPC-4761 Type VII resin fill and copper cap**, including ground decoupler/EP vias and the twelve 0.30 mm radio thermal holes. Component lead holes remain open. No controlled impedance is specified or claimed; see the fabrication notes for return-path and supplier review.

The native PCB now encodes the **requested nominal stackup**, replacing default finish/copper/dielectric metadata: **ENIG; outer copper 35 µm, inner copper 17.5 µm; dielectric gaps 0.120 / 1.255 / 0.120 mm** from front to back. Copper plus dielectrics total **1.600 mm excluding soldermask coatings**, nominal 0.010 mm each side. **Green soldermask and white silkscreen** are selected. This is the requested construction, not an unspecified default stock stackup; the supplier must quote actual materials, cured dielectric thickness and finished tolerances. No controlled-impedance order or qualification is claimed. See the nominal layer table in the fabrication notes. J6's ten open finished PTH lead holes are **0.70 mm with 1.00 mm copper lands**, nominal 0.15 mm annular ring; do not fill/cap them.

U6 placement origin is (65,7) mm, with antenna overhang approximately **6.1 mm beyond top Edge.Cuts y=0**. Its antenna copper keepout lies outside the PCB; maintain antenna clearance in the panel, assembly fixture and enclosure. Three 2.7 mm M2.5 NPTH mounting holes are centered at **(4,4), (4,71), (56,70) mm**. They are mechanical features, not DNP parts.
