## Test scope and required inputs

PCBWay shall assemble all **115 real component references in 53 BOM groups**, including J5/J6/J7 THT headers and J1 shell stakes. The 128 footprints include 13 excluded bare H/TP features, not DNP components. Assembly inspection and agreed basic electrical tests do not depend on application firmware. **Firmware-dependent factory testing is optional** and begins only after the owner supplies compatible RP/ESP images, hashes/versions, flashing commands, fixtures, scripts and acceptance limits. No firmware binary or preinstalled ESP-AT build is promised.

No firmware has been built or supplied. No customer-soldered/DNP components are planned; bare TP/H features are excluded from BOM. Exact-MPN PCBWay availability cannot be guaranteed without a quote, and neither a written test plan nor complete connectivity demonstrates functional performance.

Recommended first-article equipment: current-limited 5 V source, controllable USB host/analyzer, DMM, oscilloscope with short-ground probing, programmable system load, safe single-cell battery simulator capable of sourcing/sinking charge current, controlled NTC-resistance fixture, compatible SWD probe and 3.3 V UART adapter, logic analyzer, microSD card and I²C accessory. A real protected pack is used only after polarity and charger behavior have been checked. Never charge a bench supply that cannot safely sink current.

Record board serial/revision, released BOM/PCB revision, as-built substitutions, ambient temperature, fixture/instrument settings, supply limits, firmware hashes and observed results. Measurements should distinguish raw USB `VBUS_RAW`, fused `VBUS`, battery `VBAT`, power-path `VSYS`, +3V3 and +1V1.

## 2. Controlled power bring-up without application firmware

Start with no real battery, no SD/accessory, ESP held in reset, and RGB data low. Use a current-limited source with a limit appropriate to the selected fixture and USB mode. Check for abnormal current, heating or oscillation before increasing available current. Applying a powered probe/programmer must not bypass the intended main power path.

| Measurement | Target / requirement |
|---|---|
| VBUS_RAW / VBUS | Nominal USB5 V; inspect fuse drop and both connector orientations |
| VSYS on valid USB | Approximately **4.4 V**, TI regulation range about 4.3–4.5 V under valid conditions |
| +3V3 enabled | Nominal **3.296 V** from R42/R43; qualify accuracy/ripple across automatic PWM/PFM operation, load and temperature |
| MAIN_PG | Low before valid output, releases after regulation; R40 pull-up then enables U7 |
| +1V1 | Nominal **1.100 V**, U1 DVDD normal range **1.05–1.16 V**; measure startup and load transients |
| MAIN_VAUX | Internal U5 bias node with C42 only; no external load or accidental SYS connection |
| BQ EN1/EN2 at reset | Both low due to R7/R41; USB100 selected |
| ESP_EN | Low at startup, R20 pull-down; no unwanted radio start |
| SW5 off | U5/main rails disabled; U10 off and LED_VDD discharged through QOD, D1 dark; charger/VSYS remains connected |

Measure rail timing with MAIN_PG and U7 enable; check the RP external-core configuration supplies VIN49/AVDD46 together from +3V3. Before firmware sets HIZ, RP's unused internal regulator can consume approximately 400 µA. Test off-state leakage/back-power with VBAT remaining connected, particularly U9's high-impedance battery divider, UART adapters, J5 and the switched LED_VDD/RGB data interface. Do not equate SW5 off with zero pack drain.

Confirm C32/C33 **combined effective capacitance >22 µF** at operating bias after tolerance/temperature review, using Murata data and measurement where appropriate. Their 44 µF nominal sum is not a measured result. Observe startup, PFM ripple and input-current bursts before releasing the core/radio load.

### LED power-switch and SW5-off test

Inspect U10 TPS22919DCKR SC70-6: **1 IN=VSYS, 2 GND, 3 ON=PWR_EN, 4 NC floating, 5 QOD=LED_VDD, 6 VOUT=LED_VDD**. Check C45 1 µF on VSYS input and D1/C24/C36 on LED_VDD. With SW5 on, measure LED_VDD following VSYS through U10, and confirm the main rail remains nominal 3.296 V. The switch does not guarantee LED_VDD≥3.3 V at low battery.

**U10 schematic model:** local symbol pin5 QOD is typed **passive** to represent TI's explicitly permitted direct tie to VOUT/pin6 (LED_VDD). The stock `open_collector` type produces an ERC conflict with the power-output pin6 for this permitted connection. Only the schematic electrical type is changed: pin numbers, package pinout and footprint are unchanged. **No ERC exclusions are used.** [TI TPS22919 pin-functions table](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps22919.pdf).

With approved test firmware/fixture, latch a visible RGB color, then turn SW5 off without relying on a blanking command. Verify U10 ON goes low, LED_VDD falls through the QOD discharge path, and the LED stays dark. Measure discharge timing/current and check for back-power from RGB DIN or attached equipment, both USB-powered and battery-powered. Confirm SW5 re-enable gives the expected LED supply/startup; retain loaded-BAT≤3.6 V blanking with hysteresis. These are required first-article tests, not completed results. [TI TPS22919 primary datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps22919.pdf).

## 3. USB and charger input policy

Use owner-supplied test firmware or an approved temporary control fixture to exercise EN pins. Do not back-drive RP GPIOs with a powered source without an approved isolation/setup.

| GP16 EN1 | GP17 EN2 | Mode | Test |
|---:|---:|---|---|
| 0 | 0 | USB100 | Reset/startup input limit approximately90–100 mA; ensure firmware-controlled loads remain within host budget |
| 1 | 0 | USB500 | Assert only after host configuration allows it; limit approximately450–500 mA |
| 0 | 1 | ILIM | Verify resistor-mode limit ~497 mA if intentionally exercised; avoid this state during normal sequencing |
| 1 | 1 | Suspend | Verify input behavior against TI and USB host requirements; firmware must handle system power loss without battery |

Test enumeration/configuration, bus reset, suspend/resume, USB unplug/replug and both cable orientations. Check separate CC1/CC2 Rd and D+/D− behavior. Verify that losing configuration restores the allowed load policy. BQ mode selection alone does not certify USB compliance; measure current including board/system/charge consumption in each host state. On suspend with no battery, record rail collapse, reset and subsequent resume/re-enumeration behavior.

Check CHARGE_N on GP24 and POWERGOOD_N on GP25, both open-drain with +3V3 pull-ups. Verify charging, full-charge, input-invalid and fault indications against TI behavior. Test USB-only, battery-only and concurrent-source transitions without destructive reverse powering.

## 4. LiPo charger, NTC and DPPM

The supplied pack must be **protected 500 mAh 1S, 4.2 V maximum charge**, with cell-mounted 10 kΩ NTC. Owner/pack supplier must specify charge-rate permission, protection cutoffs and exact NTC curve/tolerance. No pack or NTC curve has been factory-qualified by this documentation.

1. Begin with a safe battery simulator and known TS resistor fixture. At 10 kΩ TS, expect approximately0.75 V from 75 µA bias; verify no unintended pull-up or fixed bypass. C18 is 100 nF filtering, not a replacement sensor.
2. Verify R5 3.57 kΩ nominal charge setting ~249 mA; factor limits alone give approximately223–273 mA before resistor/thermal effects. Charge current is allowed to reduce under USB100, DPPM or thermal regulation. Use pack-permitted limits as the final acceptance criterion.
3. Verify 4.2 V charge regulation against TI **4.16–4.23 V** limits and the pack specification. ITERM NC gives nominal10% termination in USB500 and3.3% in USB100; TMR NC gives nominal30 min precharge /5 h fast-charge timers. Test with an appropriate simulator/long-run setup, not a briefly observed LED/status change.
4. Simulate TS hot/cold, open and short; charging should inhibit as specified. Bias limits72–78 µA, hot threshold270–330 mV, cold2.0–2.2 V must be combined with the actual NTC curve. Nominal window is approximately **0–50 °C**, not guaranteed for an arbitrary generic10 kΩ thermistor. Validate sensor faults/startup on the real cable.
5. In a controlled chamber or safe thermal fixture, verify charging behavior near the specified pack temperature limits. Distinguish cell temperature from charger/core-regulator junction temperature; a board ambient sensor is not a valid cell-safety substitute.
6. Apply radio-equivalent/SD-equivalent system load while charging. Observe DPPM reducing charge before battery supplementation, SYS droop, input-current limit and battery current direction. A 250 mA setting does not promise that current concurrently with a heavily loaded system.
7. Check pack protection separately using the supplier's safe procedure. BQ24074 is not overdischarge/short-circuit protection; firmware/RGB blanking is not cell protection.

## 5. Main regulator and thermal qualification

Exercise +3V3 from USB-powered SYS and decreasing battery-derived SYS, including the **BAT2.8 V already-running** endpoint. Measure the actual U5 VIN after BQ/FET drop rather than substituting BAT for SYS. Test nominal loads and at least the intended **1 A total 3V3 qualification load** where the protected pack, wiring and fixture safely support it. A 500 mAh pack must have an explicitly suitable discharge-current rating.

**Cold startup at BAT2.8 V is not a TPS63070 guarantee.** TI requires VIN≥3.0 V while VOUT<3.0 V; separately characterize cold starts near this boundary and record the lowest repeatable startup voltage. Restart after SW5 off, battery reconnect, pack-protection recovery, USB removal and brownout. Do not pass a full-range cold-start requirement on the basis of a running discharge sweep.

Check ripple, load steps and recovery at minimum actual SYS, near buck/boost crossover and maximum intended load, with U5 PS/SYNC high/automatic mode as built. Observe input/source impedance and output ringing to validate stability with L1 **1.0 µH** and derated C32/C33. Use scope probes with minimal loop area; do not mistake probe pickup for rail ringing. Confirm no sustained oscillation or excessive undershoot affecting MCU/ESP operation.

Measure U5, U4, L1 and U7 temperature at sustained worst intended load and agreed ambient conditions. U7 dissipates roughly(3.296−1.1)×Icore; its SOT23 500 mA rating is not a guarantee of continuous high dissipation. The full-board load must include core-LDO input current, radio peaks, SD bursts, flash/PSRAM, external J4/J5 load and RGB. Establish thermal acceptance from component/pack ratings and intended enclosure, not the headline switch-current limits.

| Interface | Connections / action |
|---|---|
| J6 RP SWD | 1 +3V3 reference, 2 SWDIO, 3/5/9 GND, 4 SWCLK, 10 RUN; 6/7/8 NC, pin7 physically present |
| RP BOOTSEL | Hold SW2, toggle SW1/reset or power-cycle; confirm ROM USB boot before programming U2 |
| J7 ESP UART0 | 1 GND, 2 +3V3 reference, **3 ESP TX→adapter RX**, **4 ESP RX←adapter TX**, 5 BOOT, 6 EN |
| ESP download straps | GPIO8 high, GPIO9 low when EN rises; preserve required hold timing; keep RP GP18/19 Hi-Z during external programming |

Use 3.3 V UART logic; avoid parallel target power sources. Verify flashed image identity/readback if ordered. A passing ROM boot/download check is not an application-function pass.

RP test firmware requirements:

- Use the external-core configuration and protected POWMAN **VREG.HIZ** write; verify it takes effect and remains appropriate across power states.
- Initialize FLASH_CS on dedicated QSPI_SS and **PSRAM_CS on GP0/QMI CS1n**; both selects must remain independent. Shared clock passes R16 33 Ω.
- Honor PSRAM **150 µs stable-supply initialization**, documented reset66h/99h and interface-mode restoration on warm MCU reset. Review early shared-bus activity against PSRAM startup conditions. Limit linear clocks to84 MHz; do not assume133 MHz at3.3 V.
- Configure QMI CE-low maximum, CE-high recovery, burst/wrap mode and transaction splitting from the chosen PSRAM timing. Standard CE-low maximum8 µs, applicable extended-temperature case3 µs, CE-high minimum18 ns. Verify cold/warm reset and flash XIP escape/recovery.
- Start QSPI at **≤31.25 MHz** and SD SPI at **≤12.5 MHz after the required low-speed card initialization**. These are recommended initial ceilings pending first-article signal-integrity/error-rate tests, not a supplied firmware setting or guaranteed maximum performance. Inspect clocks/data/CS waveforms on the actual F/B paths and raise speed only after validating margins under concurrent radio/SD load, voltage and temperature variation.
- Initialize SPI1 **GP10SCK/11MOSI/12MISO**, software CS GP13, detect GP15; I²C0 GP4/5; UART1 GP20/21 with CTS22/RTS23. Do not copy the obsolete pin allocations from early research.
- Hold ESP_EN low until rails/current budget permit; set BOOT control to released/open-drain state for normal boot. Verify EN voltage/timing including R22 1 kΩ, R20 pull-down and C21.
- Calibrate ADC readings, disable ADC GPIO pulls, implement USB current-state policy and conservative **BAT≤3.6 V loaded RGB blanking** with hysteresis.

## 8. ADC measurement and fault recovery

Sweep battery simulator voltage and compare DMM VBAT with BAT_SENSE, U9 BAT_BUF and BAT_ADC. R10/R11 are470 kΩ1%; C19 input RC is23.5 ms, so allow approximately120 ms after steps before a settled reading. R38/C41 output RC is100 ns. Measure buffer offset, resistor scale error, noise and ADC/reference error under radio/SD activity; define calibrated thresholds and hysteresis from measured results.

VBUS_ADC is a separate unbuffered1 MΩ/330 kΩ divider, nominal scale330/1330≈0.2481, with100 nF. Its high source impedance and RP leakage can cause meaningful DC error; the capacitor does not remove leakage error. Treat it as a qualified/calibrated input indication, not an independently accurate USB voltage meter.

Test cable disconnect, missing/shorted NTC, protected-pack cutoff/recovery, repeated resets, firmware boot failure, SD removal and externally connected programmer/accessory power. Record unintended back-power, radio activation, charger state or LED illumination. Fault recovery must leave current-limit controls and boot straps in their intended safe default states.
