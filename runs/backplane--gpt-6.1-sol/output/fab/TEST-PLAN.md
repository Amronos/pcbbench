# First-article acceptance plan

These are proposed acceptance checks, not measured prototype results. Use current-limited supplies, a protected battery simulator where appropriate, and an independent meter. Do not ship a first article with a short, wrong polarity, missing connector, or unverified power rail.

1. Inspect all 118 fitted references against BOM and assembly drawing, including every through-hole socket. Verify RP2350 silicon revision and RF-module MPN. Inspect QFN/SON hidden joints and USB-C contacts. Check no unintended copper under the overhanging ESP antenna.
2. With power removed, check VBUS, VSYS, 3V3, 1V1, LED_5V, and VBAT for shorts to GND. Allow capacitors to charge while measuring resistance; do not prescribe a misleading fixed resistance threshold for semiconductor rails.
3. Apply 5.0 V to USB with a 100 mA limit initially and no battery. At reset radio and SD should be off. Verify nominal 3V3 (3.20–3.40 V), core 1V1 (1.06–1.14 V), and LED boost (4.75–5.25 V) without oscillation or excess heating. Confirm SD_3V3 is off. Investigate any current-limit cycling. Check RP USB ROM enumeration and BOOT/RESET operation.
4. Use a battery simulator with a 10 kΩ thermistor between J2 NTC and GND, with correct J2 polarity. Check operation from 3.0–4.2 V with USB removed. Check seamless USB insertion/removal and no backfeed into USB. For charge-current testing, grant the USB budget through control GPIOs and use a supply rated for it. Verify dynamic input limiting, approximately 499 mA programmed battery charge current when the power budget allows, 4.2 V charge regulation, charge termination, status signals, and reduced charge current as system load rises. Characterize charger temperature and thermistor hot/cold cutoff using the BQ24074 datasheet and the actual pack's NTC curve. A 10 kΩ substitute is for room-temperature bench tests only.
5. Firmware-assisted RP test: use board-specific Pico SDK configuration, external VREG.HIZ policy, 16 MB flash geometry, and GPIO0 PSRAM CS. Exercise all flash/PSRAM memory patterns, both cores, watchdog, SWD, and ADC. Compare battery ADC to a meter at 3.0, 3.7, and 4.2 V; account for divider/reference tolerances.
6. Firmware-assisted USB/power test: respect 100 mA before configuration, set 500 mA only after configuration, and suspend the charger input appropriately. Measure actual reset, configured, and suspend currents. Verify radio/card sequencing; ensure SPI pins do not back-power an unpowered card. Exercise SD insertion/detect, read/write/CRC with several cards, beginning at initialization speed and qualifying up to 24 MHz.
7. Flash a compatible official ESP32-C3-WROOM-02 ESP-AT archive using J3 UART0. Use UART1 pins IO6/7/5/4 with RTS/CTS and test AT command response. Demonstrate Wi-Fi association/data and BLE advertisement/connection with a test host. Measure radio peak current and verify no 3V3/core droop or resets. Check reception with the intended enclosure and battery positions.
8. Test Qwiic I²C at 100 and 400 kHz with a 3.3 V peripheral, expansion pins, buttons, and RGB red/green/blue at limited brightness. Check current and thermal behavior under the intended combination of radio, SD, LED, and expansion loads.

Record supply current, rail voltages, firmware versions, silicon/module lot details, temperatures, USB results, and pass/fail for every first-article function. USB full-speed integrity, RF performance, and maximum usable load are qualification items beyond ERC/DRC.
