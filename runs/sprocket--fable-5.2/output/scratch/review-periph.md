# Peripheral block review, RP2350 dev board

Reviewed from `scratch/rp2350-devboard.net`, cross-checked with the schematic, the PCB pad nets and the datasheets named below. No must-fix error found. Four should-fix items, seven notes.

## Findings

| # | Severity | Block | Finding | Evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Should fix | Qwiic J3, 3V3 header J7.1 | A powered Qwiic device (or anything feeding J7.1) drives +3V3 while the board is off. That puts U5 TLV75733P at VOUT > VIN, above its absolute maximum, and reverse current flows through the pass FET body diode into /VSYS (C2 10 µF, C4 1 µF). | J3.2 and J7.1 sit directly on +3V3 with U5.5 (OUT). U5.1 (IN) is /VSYS. TLV757P datasheet 7.1.4: reverse current occurs when "VOUT > VIN + 0.3V", which exceeds the absolute maximum, and "use external protection". | Add a small Schottky from +3V3 (anode) to /VSYS (cathode) as in TLV757P figure 7-3. A cuttable jumper in the J3.2 line is the cheaper alternative, but it leaves J7.1 exposed. |
| 2 | Should fix | RGB LED D1 | WS2812B-2020 runs from 3.3 V, below the rated supply range of the classic part. | D1.4 on +3V3. Worldsemi WS2812B-2020 V1.3, page 3: supply voltage +3.7 V to +5.3 V, VIH 2.7 V specified at VDD = 5 V only. The newer WS2812B-2020-V6 datasheet states "3.3 V power supply is supported". | Keep the circuit and fix the BOM to a 3.3 V rated version (WS2812B-2020-V6 or later, same pinout), or move D1.4 to /VSYS. On /VSYS the LED draws about 0.6 mA quiescent when the LDO is disabled, so I prefer the BOM change. |
| 3 | Should fix | Debug J4 | No series resistors on SWCLK and SWDIO. | /SWCLK is J4.1 to U1.24 only, /SWDIO is J4.3 to U1.25 only. Raspberry Pi 3-pin Debug Connector Specification, "Pinout": 100 Ω at the target IC pins, "not strictly required but highly recommended". Pico 2 W has R19 and R18, 100 Ω. | Add 2 x 100 Ω 0402 in series, placed at U1 pins 24 and 25. |
| 4 | Should fix | microSD J5 | Hot insert can dip +3V3. The only local reservoir is C27 4.7 µF 0402 (about 2.5 µF at 3.3 V bias) plus C28 100 nF, and U5 with its 10 µF is about 30 mm away. The SD specification allows up to 5 µF of card capacitance. Charge sharing with the whole rail (about 12 µF effective, my estimate) gives a dip to roughly 3.0 V for a typical 1 µF card and down to about 2.4 V for a 5 µF card. RM2 VIN minimum is 3.0 V and the RP2350 and flash share the rail. | J5.4 on +3V3, C27 and C28 2.1 mm from pad 4 in the PCB. RM2 datasheet 2.6: VIN 3.0 V to 4.8 V. | Change C27 to 22 µF 0603 at J5.4. If a reset on insert still shows up in testing, add a 0.47 Ω resistor or a ferrite bead between +3V3 and J5.4 with the capacitors on the socket side. |
| 5 | Note | RM2 U7, pico-sdk | The stock `pico2_w` board file works for the radio pins but is wrong elsewhere: 4 MB flash against 16 MB here, VSYS sense on GPIO29, VBUS sense on WL_GPIO2 and SMPS control on WL_GPIO1. U7.17 and U7.18 are open, so a VBUS read returns noise. | U7.17 GPIO2 and U7.18 GPIO1 unconnected. Pico 2 W sheet 1 uses them for VBUS sense and PS. | Ship a board header copied from `pico2_w.h` with `PICO_FLASH_SIZE_BYTES` 16 MB and the VSYS and VBUS definitions removed. No hardware change. |
| 6 | Note | RM2 U7, supply | On battery the 3V3 rail falls with VSYS once VSYS is under 3.3 V plus dropout. RM2 loses RF performance below 3.2 V. USB input is limited to 500 mA (U4 EN1 high, EN2 low), and Wi-Fi transmit (up to 320 mA) plus SD write plus RGB LED can reach that without a battery. | RM2 datasheet 2.6. TLV757P dropout 425 mV maximum at 1 A. /CHG_EN1 = VBUS/2 through R8 and R9, U4.5 EN2 on GND. | For the power reviewer: confirm the 3V3 budget and LDO heating at (VSYS - 3.3 V) x load. |
| 7 | Note | microSD J5 | Card detect switch is not used, so firmware must poll for a card. | J5.9 DET_B and J5.10 DET_A unconnected. | Leave it, or route DET to a spare GPIO with a pull-up and tie the other switch pin to GND. |
| 8 | Note | microSD J5 | In SPI mode the chip select is a software GPIO. | DAT3/CS is GPIO15, whose SPI1 function is TX. SPI1 CSn is on GPIO13 (DAT1). | None. SD libraries drive CS as a GPIO. |
| 9 | Note | User LED D4 | The LED works only after the cyw43 driver is running. 1 kΩ instead of the 470 Ω on Pico 2 W gives about 1.2 mA with a 2.1 V green LED, inside the 2 mA GPIO rating. An InGaN green LED (Vf about 3 V) would stay dark. | /WL_GPIO0: U7.8 to R29 to D4.2 (A), D4.1 (K) on GND. Pico 2 W R3 470 Ω. | Pick a green LED with Vf of 2.2 V or less. |
| 10 | Note | J3, J4, J5 | No ESD protection on the user-touchable connectors. The RP2350 pads are rated 2 kV HBM. | J3.3, J3.4, J4.1, J4.3 and J5 signals go straight to U1. RP2350 datasheet 14.9.2. | Acceptable for a dev board. The 100 Ω resistors of finding 3 help on J4. |
| 11 | Note | Outside scope, GPIO28 | With VSYS present and the LDO disabled, VBAT/2 reaches the unpowered ADC pin through 100 kΩ. ADC pads are not fault tolerant. Current is about 16 µA, so I see no damage risk. | /VBAT_SENSE: R13 to +BATT, U1.42. Pico 2 W sheet 1 note: "ADC GPIO pins have diode to VDDIO". | None required. |

## Checked and found correct

- RM2 U7 symbol and footprint: all 21 pin numbers and names match RM2 datasheet table 2, and pad positions match Raspberry Pi's own KiCad footprint.
- RM2 hookup against RM2 table 1 and Pico 2 W sheet 2: WL_ON (U7.12 and U7.13) on GPIO23, DI (U7.5) direct on GPIO24, DO (U7.6) through R20 470 Ω, nIRQ (U7.10) through R21 10 kΩ, CS (U7.9) on GPIO25, SCLK (U7.3) on GPIO29, VDDIO and VIN on +3V3, six GND pins, no pull resistors, same as Pico 2 W R22 and R23.
- RM2 decoupling: C24 4.7 µF and C25 100 nF at VIN, C26 100 nF at VDDIO, 2 mm to 4 mm from the pads.
- microSD J5 pin order 1 DAT2, 2 DAT3/CS, 3 CMD, 4 VDD, 5 CLK, 6 VSS, 7 DAT0, 8 DAT1, shield pads on GND. I checked this against the SD contact order and the stock KiCad part, not the Hirose drawing.
- SPI mode: SPI1 with SCK GPIO10, TX GPIO11 (CMD), RX GPIO12 (DAT0), CS GPIO15 as GPIO.
- SDIO by PIO: CLK GPIO10, CMD GPIO11, DAT0 to DAT3 on GPIO12 to GPIO15, consecutive, and CLK = DAT0 - 2 as the common PIO SDIO drivers expect. 10 kΩ pull-ups R24 to R28 on CMD and DAT0 to DAT3, none on CLK.
- WS2812B-2020 D1: 1 DOUT, 2 GND, 3 DIN, 4 VDD matches the Worldsemi pin table. C29 100 nF is 1.1 mm from pad 4. DIN at 3.3 V logic meets VIH, and DIN never exceeds VDD because both come from +3V3. I did not compare the footprint's physical pad positions with the datasheet drawing.
- Qwiic J3: 1 GND, 2 3V3, 3 SDA, 4 SCL, pin 1 on the left looking into the opening. GPIO4 is I2C0 SDA and GPIO5 is I2C0 SCL. R22 and R23 2.2 kΩ to +3V3.
- Debug J4: 1 SWCLK, 2 GND, 3 SWDIO on SM03B-SRSS-TB, as in the Raspberry Pi specification.
- Buttons: BOOT pulls QSPI_SS low through R17 1 kΩ, RESET pulls RUN low with R18 10 kΩ to +3V3. There is no user button.
- Unpowered and partly powered states: every peripheral shares +3V3 with RP2350 IOVDD, so no signal can exceed a supply by more than 0.5 V inside the board. A debug probe or a Qwiic device driving SWCLK, SWDIO, SDA or SCL into the unpowered board is within ratings, because these are fault-tolerant pads rated 3.63 V at IOVDD = 0 V (RP2350 datasheet table 1680). The 3V3 supply pin is the exception, see finding 1. WL_ON stays low in reset through the CYW43439 internal pull-down.
