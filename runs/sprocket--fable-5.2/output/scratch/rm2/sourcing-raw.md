## Notes

- Rows with product links reflect the scraped seller product pages. Mouser's link is a search that redirected to its product page; a direct product URL was blocked on a later attempt. Its page showed an error in the interactive buy box, but the static price and stock tables were visible. Newark also showed some network errors while displaying its stock and pricing.
- Pimoroni's rendered product page showed its cut-tape description but not price or stock in the scraper. Its [product data](https://shop.pimoroni.com/products/raspberry-pi-radio-module.js) showed price 300, available true and SKU SC1169; the store's [cart data](https://shop.pimoroni.com/cart.js) identified GBP. The VAT treatment was not established.
- BerryBase's product page says unavailable. Search results that show quantities alongside the item can include stale or recommended-product stock and were not used.
- "No matching product page found" does not prove that a seller never stocks it. The Raspberry Pi product page's reseller list did not render in the scraper.
- Farnell Japan search snippets, unverified because the product page was blocked, suggested order code 4737257, ¥685.40 at 1+ excluding tax, and 922 in UK stock. Do not rely on these figures without checking the product page.
- Manufacturer list price is $4 per the [Raspberry Pi product page](https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/radio-module-2/). A seller's discontinued listing does not establish that the manufacturer has discontinued RM2; Raspberry Pi says production will continue until at least January 2036. DigiKey says US tariffs may apply at checkout.
